## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/scan-search.svg" width="20" height="20" /> Overview

**ForenZA** is a forensic intelligence platform designed to support investigators in managing, processing, and organising digital evidence collected through drone-assisted investigations.

The platform aims to streamline the journey from raw aerial footage to structured forensic evidence through an integrated investigation workflow.

## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/workflow.svg" width="20" height="20" /> Core Workflow

```text
DRONE CAPTURE

     |
     v

     |
     v

     |
     v
INVESTIGATION REPORTS
```
