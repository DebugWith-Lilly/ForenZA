### Forensic Intelligence & Evidence Management

ForenZA is a forensic intelligence platform designed to support investigators in managing, processing, and organising digital evidence collected through drone-assisted investigations.

## Features

- \<img src="[https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/folder-search.svg](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/folder-search.svg)" width="18" height="18" /> **Case Management** — Create and manage investigations.
- \<img src="[https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/radar.svg](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/radar.svg)" width="18" height="18" /> **Drone Live Feed** — Access drone surveillance footage.
- \<img src="[https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/brain-circuit.svg](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/brain-circuit.svg)" width="18" height="18" /> **AI Processing** — Analyse and categorise captured evidence.
- \<img src="[https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/files.svg](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/files.svg)" width="18" height="18" /> **Evidence Management** — Organise investigation evidence.
