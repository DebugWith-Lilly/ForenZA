## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/scan-search.svg" width="20" height="20" /> Problem Statement

Forensic investigations often involve collecting and analysing large volumes of digital evidence, including photographs and video footage. Managing this information across different locations and formats can make investigations time-consuming and difficult to organise.

Although drone technology provides investigators with valuable aerial perspectives, transforming raw footage into structured, accessible forensic evidence remains a challenge.

There is a need for an integrated solution that connects drone-assisted surveillance, AI-powered analysis, and digital evidence management to streamline forensic investigations.

## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/radar.svg" width="20" height="20" /> Our Solution — ForenZA

### From Aerial Surveillance to Forensic Intelligence.

ForenZA is a drone-assisted forensic intelligence platform designed to help investigators capture, process, and organise digital evidence.

The platform brings together five key capabilities:

- <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/folder-search.svg" width="18" height="18" /> **Case Management** — Create and manage forensic investigation cases.
- <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/radar.svg" width="18" height="18" /> **Drone Live Feed** — Access drone surveillance footage through a dedicated interface.
- <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/brain-circuit.svg" width="18" height="18" /> **AI Processing** — Analyse captured visual evidence and generate preliminary descriptions.
- <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/folder-archive.svg" width="18" height="18" /> **Evidence Management** — Organise photographs, videos, and investigation-related evidence.
- <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/file-chart-line.svg" width="18" height="18" /> **AI Summaries** — Generate structured preliminary summaries to support investigations.
